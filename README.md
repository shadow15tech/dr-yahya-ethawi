# Dr. Yahya Ethawi — Professional Website

A premium personal professional website for **Dr. Yahya Ethawi**, presenting his
professional identity, clinical and academic journey, healthcare expertise,
innovation work, and collaboration interests.

The website is designed to position Dr. Yahya at the intersection of:

- Clinical Medicine
- Neonatology
- Pediatrics
- Healthcare Innovation
- Medical Technology
- Research
- Artificial Intelligence
- Connected Healthcare

---

## Project Overview

This website is not intended to function as a generic hospital or clinic website.

Its primary purpose is to communicate:

1. Dr. Yahya Ethawi's professional identity
2. His clinical and academic journey
3. His areas of expertise
4. His healthcare innovation vision
5. MENAH HIS as part of the broader innovation ecosystem
6. NOOR EMAD as an innovation project in development
7. His interest in meaningful healthcare, research and technology collaboration

The website follows a premium clinical + scientific visual direction with a
dark navy foundation, restrained cyan accents, structured typography and
controlled motion.

## Innovation Presentation

### MENAH HIS

MENAH HIS is presented as part of the healthcare information and
smart-hospital innovation direction.

The concept connects areas such as:

- Clinical context
- Monitoring
- Physiological signals
- Laboratory information
- Imaging
- Documentation
- Processing
- Presentation
- AI interpretation
- Clinical decision support
- Authorized workflows
- Outcomes and learning

The website presents this as an innovation concept and does not make
unsupported claims regarding deployment, clinical validation, regulatory
approval or commercial implementation.

---

### NOOR EMAD

NOOR EMAD is presented as a healthcare technology innovation project in
development.

Project-stated information includes a direction involving:

- Smart monitoring
- Medical information processing
- AI / medical intelligence
- Multilingual interaction
- Physiological and biological connections
- Medical-device connectivity
- Knowledge systems
- Recording capabilities
- Presenter / avatar concepts
- Future physical robotics

The website must not present NOOR EMAD as:

- A licensed doctor
- An independently practicing medical professional
- A clinically validated diagnostic system
- A regulatory-approved medical device
- A deployed hospital system
- A replacement for authorized healthcare professionals

Clinical responsibility remains with the appropriate authorized
healthcare professional.
